## Preface

Dormitory broadband usually requires account login, making it impossible to share WiFi directly using a router. This article will introduce how to configure a router (using **Padavan firmware** as an example) to break through the campus network's "one person, one account" restriction.

> **Note**: This tutorial is based on tests conducted with **China Mobile Broadband** (Pingfeng Campus). For other carriers, please refer to the methodology and configure independently.

## Preparation

- Router (Recommended flashable models like **Newifi 3**, **K2P**, etc.)
- **Immortal Breed**
- **Padavan firmware**
- School broadband account and password
- Network cable

> **Cost Estimate**: Approximately ¥150

**Purchase Recommendations**:
Can be purchased on platforms like Taobao.
* If you are technically proficient, you can purchase a **brand new, sealed** device and flash it yourself.
* If you are unfamiliar with flashing, it is usually less hassle to purchase a device that already has **Immortal Breed** and **Padavan firmware** installed.

> **Tip**: This article does not include flashing tutorials; please search for relevant information independently.

## Initial Configuration and Router Usage

> **Important**: If you find that you suddenly cannot connect to the internet after using the device normally for a period, your device may have been blocked.
> **Solution**: Enter the router backend → **External Network (WAN)** → Scroll down to find **MAC Address** → Click the `+` sign to modify your MAC address.

### 1. Unbinding Devices (Important)

First, connect to the official school WiFi `Wlan-edu`. After connecting, ignore the pop-up authentication window.

In the browser address bar, enter `192.168.210.100` to enter the **User Self-Service System**.

![登录系统界面](https://cdn.yikzero.com/markdown/images/3877436746.jpg)

```plain
账号：学号
密码：身份证后八位 (或你修改后的密码)
```

After logging in, find and click **Unbind all MAC addresses**. Once complete, disconnect from the `Wlan-edu` WiFi.

![解绑所有MAC地址](https://cdn.yikzero.com/markdown/images/127070192.jpg)

### 2. Connecting the Router

1.  Power on the router.
2.  Use a network cable to connect the wall network port (usually the prominent port dedicated to China Mobile Broadband) and the router's **WAN port** (usually the blue port).
3.  After the router boots up, connect to its WiFi (the default SSID is usually `PDCN` or `PDCN 5G`).

```plain
默认Wifi名称：PDCN 或 PDCN 5G
默认密码：1234567890
```

## Configuration Method

### 1. Logging into the Firmware Backend

After connecting to the router's WiFi, open a browser and enter: `192.168.123.1` in the address bar (the default backend address for **Padavan firmware**).

![登录弹窗](https://cdn.yikzero.com/markdown/images/3199039527.jpg)

Enter the default account and password:

```plain
账号：admin
密码：admin
```

**If you cannot log in**

> 1. Please try changing your browser (Chrome is recommended).
> 2. Please check if you are correctly connected to the router's WiFi.

### 2. VPN Configuration

After logging into the backend, find and click **External Network (WAN)** in the left menu.

On the **External Network (WAN)** settings page, configure as follows:

1.  **WAN Connection Type**: Select `L2TP`
2.  **VPN Server**: `192.168.113.1` (Pingfeng Campus; for other campuses, please inquire independently)
3.  **User Name**: `hzxhaXXXXXXX` (Pingfeng Campus defaults to `hzxha` + last eight digits of phone number)
4.  **Password**: `XXXXXX` (Pingfeng Campus defaults to last six digits of phone number)

![VPN配置界面](https://cdn.yikzero.com/markdown/images/4129165676.jpg)

To prevent detection by the carrier (may not be effective), you can make the following setting:

* Find the **Route forwarding without decreasing TTL** option and change it to **For all packets**.

![修改为针对所有包](https://cdn.yikzero.com/markdown/images/3452305555.jpg)

Click **Apply** at the bottom of the page to save the configuration.

### 3. Logging into the Campus Network

After saving the settings, wait a moment. Open any webpage; theoretically, it should automatically jump to the **Campus Network login page**.

> **Tip**: If it does not redirect automatically, please manually enter `192.168.210.111` in your browser.

![跳转到这个页面](https://cdn.yikzero.com/markdown/images/3204033419.jpg)

Enter your account and password:

```plain
账号：学号
密码：身份证后八位 (或你修改后的密码)
```

![登录成功页面](https://cdn.yikzero.com/markdown/images/956320776.jpg)

After successful login, you can share the network through the router.
